1 · Desk

            
Click the desk's 4 corners in the camera view: front-left, front-right, back-right, back-left (front = your side). Then its real size.

            

              
              
            

            

            
Training uses this size too: half the sim desks become your desk's size.

            
2 · Vector

            
It finds Vector by itself: the Vector-sized thing on the desk that isn't desk (any desk colour). If it picks the wrong thing, click on Vector. Click its green backpack light so it knows which way it faces.

            

            
Markers (best): two different colours on Vector, one at the front, one at the back. Click each once.

            

            

              
              
              
            

            

            
Charger

            
Click the charger, then a spot just in front of it (the side Vector drives up to). Going home then drives straight there and lets Vector dock.

            

            
            

            
3 · Real run

            
Scores a real drive on your desk like the sim exam: desk covered, edge saves (the reflex had to step in), falls. Pick a camera-trained desk policy, or score yourself driving in Teleop.
